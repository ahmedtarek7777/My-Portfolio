Algorithm lab

Tap cells to paint. Green = start, pink = destination, gray = wall, purple = weight. Blue cells have been explored; gold cells form the path.

BFS finds the fewest steps and ignores weights. Dijkstra finds the lowest total traversal cost, including weighted cells. Movement is up, down, left and right.
